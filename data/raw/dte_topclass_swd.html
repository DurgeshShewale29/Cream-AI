Scholarship For Top Class Education for Students with Disabilities

The Scholarship For Top Class Education for Students with Disabilities (Scholarship For Top Class Education for Students with Disabilities) has been implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India, New Delhi, through the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, for students pursuing higher education (undergraduate and postgraduate degree/diploma courses) in institutions designated by the Central Government for students with disabilities from the year 2015-16. For this scheme दिव्यांगाचे Disability level of  40 % or more and annual family income (both parents combined) is fixed up t0 Rs, 8.00 lakhs Directorate of Technical Education, Maharashtra State has been declared as the nodal office in the state of Maharashtra for this scheme.

Scholarship Amount -

Under this scheme, the following scholarships are provided to eligible students who come for new scholarships and renewals.

Sr.No.

Scholarship components

Amount

A)

Reimbursement of admission fees and academic fees payable to the institution/university

Up to Rs.1,90,000/- per annum (subject to actual amount)

B)

Miscellaneous/Miscellaneous Allowances

i)

Library fees, sports fees, medical examinations and other necessary fees payable by the institution/university/board

Up to Rs.10,000/- per annum (subject to actual amount)

ii)

Reimbursement of the cost of purchasing computers/laptops and their related spare parts

Rs. 45,000/- payable once in the first year of the full course.

iii)

Reimbursement of the cost of purchasing aids and assistive devices, including software, required for the specific disability of selected candidates.

Rs. 30,000/- payable once in the first year of the full course.

C)

Maintenance/Preservation Allowance

Rs. 3,000/- per month for students staying in hostels,

Rs. 1,500/- per month for day-scholar students

D)

Special allowances (depending on the type of disability, such as reading allowance, protection allowance, assistance allowance, etc.)

Rs. 2,000/- per month.

E)

Book Allowance

Rs. 5,000/- per annum

The sanctioned amount of these schemes is deposited directly into the bank account of the students by the Central Government under Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Detailed information about the above schemes is given on the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment of the Central Government(website-www.disabilityaffairs.gov.in)/National Scholarship Portal (NSP) of the Central Government (website-www.scholarships.gov.in).ww.disabilityaffairs.gov.in ).

सदर योजना केंद्र शासनाच्या National Scholarship Portal ( NSP ) पोर्टलद्वारे ( संकेतस्थळ – www.scholarships.gov.in) ऑनलाईनरित्या राबविण्यात येते. सदर योजनेच्या लाभासाठी प्रवेशित विद्यार्थ्यांनी नवीन ( Fresh ) व मागील शैक्षणिक वर्षात शिष्यवृत्ती मंजूर झालेल्या पात्र विद्यार्थ्यानी नुतनीकरणासाठी ( Renewal ) अर्ज NSP पोर्टलवर नमूद करण्यात आलेल्या अंतिम मुदतीच्या आत ऑनलाईन अर्ज करणे अनिवार्य आहे.
